Find what was said or decided in past meetings about: $ARGUMENTS

- Search the meeting notes and transcripts you can reach (a notes connector, the channel's files, the notes folder).
- Answer in a few bullets: what was decided, by whom, when, and any follow-up that was promised.
- Quote the exact line when wording matters, and link or name each meeting you used with its date.
- If nothing matches, say where you looked. Do not guess.
