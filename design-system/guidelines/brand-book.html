Fluid agents, a calm instrument.

Omni OS is one person’s workspace for a crew of AI agents, across every client project. This book holds the rules that keep it calm.

Idea

The agents are the liquid; the workspace holds them. Everything that moves on its own is agent work. Everything you touch is a quiet, precise instrument.

Proportion

Roughly how a working screen should feel. If a pop isn’t saying something, it shouldn’t be there.

  1. Never tinted, never washed. No 8% backgrounds, no pastel pills. A pop is a dot, a stroke or a filled pill carrying ink.
  2. Selection is a thumb, not a color. The active row, tab or option sits on a sliding white thumb.
  3. Volt never sits bare on white, and vermilion is never text on white. Both become filled pills with ink text.
  4. Channels have no colors. Neutral round avatars, written #Volero.
  5. Primary actions are ink pills. Never a pop.

07

Iconography

Hand-drawn on a 24 grid with a 1.75 round stroke, in the color of the text beside them. Vendor logos stay exactly as their owners drew them.

Interface icons24 grid · 1.75 stroke · round caps
HarnessesSimple Icons, unmodified
Claude CodeClaude
CodexOpenAI
Cursor AgentCursor
  1. Logos, never letters. Wherever a harness is named, show its logo. Never CC, CX or CU.
  2. Never recolor a vendor logo with a status pop. They are their owners’ trademarks.
  3. Unicode only for keys and arrows: ⌘ ⇧ ↵ ↑ ↓.
  4. No emoji. No sparkles for “AI”.
