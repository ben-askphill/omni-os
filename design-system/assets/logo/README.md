The Omni mark (ring + satellite droplet), the "omni" wordmark, favicons and app icons. Always just "omni", no "OS" lockup.

- `omni-mark.svg`, `omni-wordmark.svg`, `favicon-16.svg`, `favicon-ring-16.svg`: single ink `#0D0D0C`, for light grounds (`canvas`, `bg`, `surface`).
- `omni-mark-white.svg`, `favicon-16-white.svg`: single ink `#FFFFFF`; `omni-wordmark-white.svg`: single ink `#ECEAE5`. For dark grounds (`chrome`, dark `canvas`).
- `omni-mark-needs.svg`: single ink `#FF4420` (`vermilion`): attention only.
- `app-icon.svg` (dark tile `#0D0D0C`, light ring) and `app-icon-light.svg` (light tile `#F3F1EC`, dark ring).
- `favicon-16*.svg` have their own geometry for 16px; `favicon-ring-16.svg` drops the droplet.
- `omni-mark-1000.jpg`: the mark as a raster.
