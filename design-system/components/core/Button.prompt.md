Pill buttons; primary is ink, needs is reserved for resolving a waiting decision.

```jsx
<Button variant="primary" icon="plus">New thread</Button>
<Button variant="needs" size="sm">Pause automation</Button>
```

variant primary | secondary | ghost | needs · size sm 28 | md 34 | lg 44 · icon (name or node) · busy · kbd. Press scales to .96.
