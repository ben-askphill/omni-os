Status marks, dots and pills: one drawing per thread state (18 in all, see StatusDot).

```jsx
<StatusDot mark="working" />
<Status mark="question" />
<StatusGlyph state="needs" />
```

Shape says who has the ball (circle agent, square outside system, diamond you, cross or triangle broken), colour repeats it, fill says how far along, a satellite says it is the crew's. A halo while the agent's turn works, a breathe while it waits on other work; nothing spins.
