The fluid Omni mark: at splash, beside the Conductor and wherever the brand should be felt. It never shows that something runs: that is StatusDot's job (PR #755).

```jsx
<OmniMark size={48} />
<OmniMark state="attention" onAcknowledge={ack} />
```

states: idle · hover · attention. thinking, delegating and report are kept for old call sites and draw idle. `time` freezes a keyframe. StaticMark for ≤24px and print; `ring` drops the droplet at 16px.
